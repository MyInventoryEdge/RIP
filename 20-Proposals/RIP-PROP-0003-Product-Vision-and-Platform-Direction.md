# RIP-PROP-0003 — Product Vision and Platform Direction

**Proposal ID:** RIP-PROP-0003  
**Title:** Product Vision and Platform Direction  
**Status:** Approved and Implemented  
**Originator:** Bob Berry and RIP design collaboration  
**Created:** 2026-07-28  
**Gatekeeper:** Bob Berry  

## 1. Problem Statement

RIP's initial constitutional frame correctly established organizational authority, governed learning, Hosts, and institutional memory. However, the repository still described RIP primarily as an organizational engineering system centered heavily on repository and engineering governance.

The organization's current understanding is broader and more coherent:

RIP is a governed organizational knowledge platform.

Leaving this understanding only in a conversation would create the exact tribal-knowledge failure RIP exists to prevent.

## 2. Origin and Provenance

This proposal originated during the architectural discussion that followed the first independent repository assessment.

The discussion clarified that:

- the Organization is the primary object;
- repository intelligence is one specialization;
- knowledge is the primary enduring asset;
- governance establishes authority;
- execution applies governed knowledge;
- conversations must be promoted into governed artifacts;
- concepts must be independent from providers;
- operations can be generated from organizational understanding;
- RIP must govern its own evolution.

## 3. Supporting Evidence

- The initial repository already identifies the organization as the source of authority.
- The existing Constitution separates learning from governance.
- The first repository assessment demonstrated the value of testing whether the repository can explain itself.
- The design conversation produced a coherent platform model that exceeded the repository's existing representation.
- Continuing to leave those decisions outside the repository would contradict RIP's institutional-memory purpose.

## 4. Proposed Change

Adopt and propagate the following direction throughout RIP:

1. RIP is a governed organizational knowledge platform.
2. The Organization is the primary object.
3. Knowledge, Governance, and Execution are foundational pillars.
4. Knowledge must retain provenance.
5. Conversations produce ideas but are not institutional memory.
6. The governed lifecycle is Conversation → Idea → Proposal → Decision → Implementation → Validation → Institutional Knowledge.
7. Business knowledge is generally supplied.
8. Technical knowledge may be inferred.
9. Concepts are modeled independently from providers.
10. Operations is generated from current organizational understanding.
11. Changes are first-class traceable organizational events.
12. AI and Hosts assist but never silently establish authority.
13. RIP must apply its own governance principles to itself.
14. The repository should not intentionally lag behind approved organizational understanding.

## 5. Constitutional Purpose

This change strengthens:

- institutional memory;
- explicit authority;
- traceability;
- deliverability;
- conceptual durability;
- self-governance;
- organizational learning.

## 6. Expected Benefit

A new engineer should be able to infer RIP's purpose, authority model, knowledge lifecycle, conceptual structure, and long-term direction from the repository alone.

Future implementation should proceed from one coherent model rather than fragmented chat context.

## 7. Risks and Tradeoffs

- The broader platform vision increases implementation scope.
- Terminology must remain disciplined to prevent the platform from becoming vague.
- Generated operations and organizational modeling require substantial future implementation.
- Some initially repository-centered language must be superseded or reframed.

## 8. Alternatives Considered

### Preserve the existing repository and defer the broader vision
Rejected because it would intentionally preserve a gap between organizational understanding and institutional memory.

### Implement the direction in staged documentation phases
Rejected because the decisions are coherent, approved, and cross-cutting. Artificial staging would duplicate work and prolong inconsistency.

### Ask an external engineer or Cody to organize the decisions
Rejected because the organization has already made the decisions. Engineers should evaluate and implement governed direction, not determine where already-approved organizational knowledge belongs.

## 12. Validation

Validation will determine whether:

- foundational documents consistently describe RIP as a governed organizational knowledge platform;
- the Organization is represented as the primary object;
- provenance, concepts, providers, Operations, Changes, and self-governance are represented;
- a new engineer can infer the approved direction without access to the originating conversation.

## 13. Reconsideration Criteria

Reconsider this direction if:

- implementation demonstrates that the Organization cannot function as the primary object;
- the knowledge/governance/execution model creates irreconcilable ambiguity;
- validated customer use shows a materially different foundational model is required.
