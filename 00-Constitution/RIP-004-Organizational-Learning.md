# RIP-004 — Organizational Learning

**Status:** Initial Draft

## Purpose

Organizational learning converts experience into durable, governed knowledge.

## Learning Loop

```text
Observe
  ↓
Collect Evidence
  ↓
Recognize Patterns
  ↓
Generate Recommendations
  ↓
Present Evidence
```

The learning loop may operate autonomously within approved boundaries.

## Governance Loop

```text
Recommendation
  ↓
Gatekeeper Review
  ↓
Approve / Reject / Defer
  ↓
Authority Updated When Approved
  ↓
Hosts Enact
  ↓
Outcome Validated
```

## Principle

The system learns. The organization governs.

## Preservation Requirement

Learning is incomplete unless its evidence, reasoning, decision, implementation, and observed outcome remain traceable.

## Failure and Experimentation

RIP does not exist to eliminate failure. It exists to prevent the needless loss of what failure teaches.

An organization SHOULD NOT have to pay twice for the same lesson.
