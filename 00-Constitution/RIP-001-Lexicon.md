# RIP-001 — Lexicon

**Status:** Initial Draft

## Authority
A currently approved organizational rule, principle, standard, or decision that governs action.

## Constitution
The highest-level body of organizational engineering authority within RIP. It defines durable principles and boundaries rather than implementation detail.

## Evidence
An observation, outcome, artifact, measurement, experience, or pattern that may inform a proposal or decision.

## Evolution Register
The permanent record of proposals and their lifecycle, decisions, rationale, implementation, validation, and reconsideration criteria.

## Gatekeeper
The authorized governance role or body that may approve, reject, defer, reopen, supersede, or withdraw proposals. A Gatekeeper may be an individual, committee, board, or other formally designated authority.

## Host
A constitutional executor that interprets and enacts approved organizational authority. A Host does not independently create authority.

## Institutional Memory
The preserved body of evidence, reasoning, decisions, outcomes, and history that allows an organization to learn without repeatedly paying for the same lesson.

## Organization
The entity whose engineering authority, history, standards, and learning are governed through RIP.

## Proposal
A traceable request to create, modify, supersede, or retire authority, standards, structures, or behavior.

## Standard
An approved implementation rule subordinate to the Constitution.

## History
The preserved narrative and record explaining how authority evolved. History informs current judgment but does not itself govern unless incorporated into current authority.
