# RIP-001 — Lexicon

**Document ID:** RIP-001  
**Title:** Lexicon  
**Status:** Ratified  
**Authority:** RIP-000 Constitution  
**Classification:** Constitutional (Normative)  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Owner:** Gatekeeper  
**Parent Authority:** RIP-000 Constitution

---

## Actor
A person, team, organizational body, Host, service, or other entity capable of performing an action within declared boundaries.

## Artifact
A durable representation of organizational knowledge, evidence, authority, reasoning, implementation, validation, or history.

## Assumption
A proposition treated as conditionally true for the purpose of interpretation or reasoning but not established as verified knowledge or authority.

## Authority
A currently approved organizational rule, principle, standard, delegation, or decision that governs action.

## Business Knowledge
Knowledge about the organization's purpose, products, customers, policies, intentions, constraints, responsibilities, and operating context. Business knowledge is generally supplied by authorized organizational sources.

## Capability
Something an organization, Actor, Host, service, or provider can reliably do within defined constraints and authority.

## Change
A traceable organizational event that modifies knowledge, authority, products, repositories, services, infrastructure, operations, standards, or behavior.

## Concept
A durable, provider-independent organizational capability, responsibility, entity, or relationship.

## Confidence
An explicit estimate of how strongly available evidence supports an interpretation or conclusion. Confidence does not create authority.

## Constitution
The highest-level body of organizational authority within RIP. It defines durable principles and boundaries rather than provider or implementation detail.

## Constraint
A condition, limitation, obligation, dependency, or boundary within which an organization or capability must operate.

## Conversation
A transient exchange that may generate understanding but is not, by itself, institutional memory or organizational authority.

## Decision
An explicit governance outcome issued by authorized authority. A decision records what was approved, rejected, deferred, withdrawn, reopened, or superseded and why.

## Discovery
A newly recognized and evidence-supported understanding of organizational reality. A Discovery may inform knowledge, an Idea, a recommendation, or a Proposal, but does not independently create authority.

## Evidence
An observation, outcome, artifact, measurement, experience, event, or pattern that may support or contradict knowledge, an interpretation, a proposal, a decision, or validation.

## Evolution Register
The permanent ledger of proposals and their lifecycle, decisions, rationale, implementation, validation, and reconsideration criteria.

## Gatekeeper
The authorized governance role or body that may approve, reject, defer, reopen, supersede, or withdraw proposals.

## Governance
The authorized process through which organizational changes, proposals, delegations, standards, and decisions become legitimate, traceable, reviewable, and evolvable.

## Governance Chronicle
The governed, non-normative record of how RIP's governance evolved, including its decisions, rationale, lineage, amendments, and milestones. RIP-006 is the canonical Governance Chronicle.

## History
The preserved narrative and record explaining how knowledge and authority evolved. History informs current judgment but does not itself govern unless incorporated into current authority.

## Host
A constitutional executor that interprets and enacts approved organizational authority. A Host does not independently create authority.

## Idea
An identified possibility, concern, opportunity, pattern, or potential change that has not yet become a formal proposal.

## Implementation
The enacted change intended to fulfill an approved decision.

## Inferred Knowledge
Knowledge derived from evidence by analysis, discovery, observation, or automated reasoning. Inferred knowledge remains distinguishable from supplied knowledge and authority.

## Institutional Knowledge
Durable organizational understanding whose provenance, context, authority state, reasoning, implementation history, and validation are preserved.

## Institutional Memory
The preserved body of knowledge, evidence, reasoning, decisions, outcomes, and history that allows an organization to learn without repeatedly paying for the same lesson.

## Interpretation
A reasoned explanation of what observed evidence may mean, including assumptions, alternatives, uncertainty, and confidence.

## Knowledge
Organizational understanding represented with context, provenance, state, relationships, uncertainty, and authority status.

## Knowledge Provenance
The traceable origin and history of knowledge, including source, time, evidence, transformations, interpretation, authorization, and later revision.

## Mission
The current expression of what an organization is seeking to accomplish in service of its Purpose. RIP-005 is the canonical statement of RIP's Mission.

## Objective
A defined outcome an organization intends to achieve. Objectives may change without changing the organization's enduring Purpose.

## Observation
A description of available evidence or organizational state that does not, by itself, assert an interpretation, recommendation, or authority.

## Operations
The current operational view of the organization generated from governed knowledge, relationships, dependencies, state, evidence, and authority.

## Organization
RIP's primary object: the entity whose people, products, repositories, services, infrastructure, knowledge, authority, operations, changes, and history are represented and governed.

## Organizational Maturity
The degree to which an organization can accurately explain what it understands, what it does not understand, what evidence supports that understanding, what uncertainty remains, and how its knowledge becomes governed action.

## Organizational Ontology
The governed set of durable concepts and relationships through which RIP represents and cultivates organizational understanding.

## Principle
A durable organizational belief or rule that guides interpretation, governance, and action.

## Proposal
A traceable request to create, modify, supersede, or retire authority, knowledge structures, standards, concepts, relationships, or behavior.

## Provider
A technology, vendor, platform, or implementation that supplies capabilities for one or more governed concepts.

## Purpose
The enduring reason an organization or capability exists.

## Reasoning
The disciplined transformation of evidence into interpretations and recommendations under constitutional governance.

## Reasoning Contract
The provider-independent requirements governing how reasoning is requested, structured, evidenced, qualified, recorded, and presented within RIP.

## Recommendation
A reasoned proposed course of action that has not become authority unless adopted through governance.

## Registry
The authoritative catalog of governed artifacts, including their identity, status, classification, authority, version, ownership, and lineage. RIP-007 is the canonical Constitutional Document Registry.

## Relationship
A meaningful and traceable connection between organizational objects, concepts, artifacts, Actors, authority, evidence, or history.

## Repository
A source of technical artifacts and evidence associated with the organization. A repository is not the primary object of RIP.

## Role
A defined responsibility, function, or authority scope that may be assigned to one or more Actors.

## Standard
An approved implementation rule subordinate to the Constitution.

## Supplied Knowledge
Knowledge explicitly provided by an authorized person, artifact, system, or organizational process.

## Technical Knowledge
Knowledge about repositories, services, dependencies, interfaces, infrastructure, configurations, providers, deployments, runtime behavior, and engineering implementation. Technical knowledge may be supplied or inferred.

## Uncertainty
An explicit representation of what is unknown, incomplete, ambiguous, disputed, weakly evidenced, or subject to alternative interpretation.

## Understanding
The organization's integrated, evidence-aware, relationship-aware, provenance-preserving account of reality, including what is known, inferred, uncertain, governed, and not yet understood.

## Validation
Recorded evidence and evaluation determining whether an implementation satisfies an approved decision and achieves its intended purpose.
