# RIP-000 — Constitution

**Status:** Initial Draft  
**Authority:** Repository Intelligence Platform Gatekeeper  
**Purpose:** Define the foundational authority, obligations, and boundaries of RIP.

## 1. Mission

RIP preserves, enacts, and continuously evolves an organization's engineering discipline through governed learning and constitutional authority.

## 2. Authority

The organization is the sole source of its engineering authority.

No Host, model, tool, repository, individual agent, or automated process may silently create organizational policy. Authority exists only when recognized through the organization's approved governance process.

## 3. Hosts

A Host is a constitutional executor.

A Host SHALL:

- interpret and enact approved organizational authority;
- identify when required authority or context is missing;
- refuse to represent an unapproved preference as policy;
- preserve traceability between actions, evidence, and authority;
- expose uncertainty rather than conceal it.

A Host SHALL NOT:

- silently create constitutional authority;
- overwrite organizational history;
- treat generated output as inherently authoritative;
- bypass the Gatekeeper.

## 4. Learning and Governance

RIP separates learning from governance.

The learning system MAY observe evidence, recognize patterns, and generate proposals.

Only the Gatekeeper MAY approve a proposal as organizational authority.

The system learns. The organization governs.

## 5. Institutional Memory

RIP SHALL preserve:

- proposals;
- evidence;
- decisions;
- decision rationale;
- implementation outcomes;
- validation results;
- reconsideration criteria.

Rejected, deferred, withdrawn, and superseded proposals SHALL remain part of the institutional record.

## 6. Evolution

Organizations SHALL preserve their history without becoming prisoners of past decisions.

Past decisions are evidence, not immutable law. Authority may evolve through the governed proposal process.

## 7. Constitutional Purpose

Every directory, artifact, protocol, field, interface, and automated behavior in RIP SHALL have an identifiable constitutional purpose.

Artifacts without a defensible purpose SHOULD be removed, deferred, or rejected.

## 8. Deliverability

RIP SHALL be built as a usable system rather than an aspirational description.

Foundational artifacts SHALL be:

- inspectable;
- traceable;
- understandable;
- versioned;
- implementable;
- transferable to another engineer or organization.

## 9. Amendment

This Constitution may be amended only through an approved RIP proposal. Every amendment SHALL retain its proposal history and decision rationale.
