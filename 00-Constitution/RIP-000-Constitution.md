# RIP-000 — Constitution

**Document ID:** RIP-000  
**Title:** Constitution  
**Status:** Ratified  
**Authority:** Organization through the Gatekeeper  
**Classification:** Constitutional (Normative)  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Owner:** Gatekeeper  
**Parent Authority:** Self-governing constitutional authority

---

## 1. Mission Authority

RIP-005 — Mission is the canonical and authoritative statement of why RIP exists.

Every governed artifact, capability, implementation, and operational activity SHALL support and remain consistent with RIP-005.

This Constitution defines the enduring authority, obligations, and boundaries through which that Mission is governed and enacted.

## 2. Primary Object

The Organization SHALL be RIP's primary object.

Repositories, products, services, people, infrastructure, operations, providers, Hosts, decisions, and history SHALL be modeled as parts of or relationships within the organization.

No implementation technology or repository SHALL silently replace the Organization as the center of the model.

Reality precedes implementation. RIP SHALL model organizational reality rather than redefine it for implementation convenience.

## 3. Authority

The organization is the sole source of its authority.

No Host, model, tool, repository, provider, person acting outside delegated authority, or automated process may silently create organizational policy.

Authority exists only when recognized through the organization's approved governance process.

Authority SHALL NOT be inferred from usefulness, confidence, technical control, repetition, age, or implementation status.

## 4. Knowledge and Understanding

RIP SHALL preserve organizational knowledge with sufficient context to understand:

- what is believed or known;
- where it came from;
- what evidence supports it;
- whether it is supplied, inferred, proposed, approved, rejected, deferred, superseded, or historical;
- who or what created it;
- who authorized it;
- how it was implemented;
- whether it was validated;
- what later evidence changed it.

Knowledge without provenance SHALL NOT be represented as trusted organizational knowledge.

Evidence precedes conclusion. Uncertainty SHALL be explicit. Significant conclusions and decisions SHALL remain explainable through their evidence, assumptions, reasoning, authority, and limitations.

Understanding precedes automation. RIP SHALL seek to understand organizational purpose, structure, authority, evidence, constraints, and uncertainty before recommending or performing consequential action.

## 5. Conversation and Institutional Memory

A conversation is not institutional memory.

Important organizational understanding SHALL be promoted from transient conversation into durable governed artifacts.

RIP SHALL support the lifecycle:

```text
Conversation
    ↓
Idea
    ↓
Proposal
    ↓
Decision
    ↓
Implementation
    ↓
Validation
    ↓
Institutional Knowledge
```

## 6. Hosts

A Host is a constitutional executor.

A Host SHALL:

- interpret and enact approved organizational authority;
- identify when required authority or context is missing;
- distinguish supplied knowledge, inferred knowledge, recommendations, and authority;
- refuse to represent an unapproved preference as policy;
- preserve traceability between actions, evidence, knowledge, reasoning, and authority;
- expose uncertainty rather than conceal it;
- operate within explicit capability boundaries.

A Host SHALL NOT:

- silently create constitutional authority;
- overwrite organizational history;
- treat generated output as inherently authoritative;
- bypass the Gatekeeper;
- define the organization's conceptual architecture through provider-specific assumptions;
- approve its own proposals or constitutional changes unless explicitly authorized through governance.

## 7. Learning, Reasoning, and Governance

RIP separates learning and reasoning from governance.

The learning system MAY observe evidence, infer technical knowledge, recognize patterns, identify inconsistencies, ask questions, and generate ideas, interpretations, recommendations, or proposals.

Reasoning SHALL proceed through distinguishable stages of observation, interpretation, and recommendation before governance, decision, or implementation.

Only authorized governance MAY convert a proposal or recommendation into organizational authority.

The system learns and reasons. The organization governs.

## 8. Institutional Memory

Institutional memory is an organizational asset.

RIP SHALL preserve:

- ideas of lasting organizational relevance;
- proposals;
- evidence;
- assumptions and reasoning where material;
- decisions;
- decision rationale;
- rejected and deferred alternatives;
- implementation outcomes;
- validation results;
- reconsideration criteria;
- provenance;
- relevant historical context.

Rejected, deferred, withdrawn, and superseded artifacts SHALL remain part of the institutional record.

RIP-006 — Governance Chronicle preserves the historical evolution, reasoning, and milestones of RIP governance without independently creating normative authority.

## 9. Concepts and Providers

RIP SHALL model durable concepts independently from provider-specific implementations.

Providers SHALL implement declared concepts and capabilities.

Provider selection SHALL NOT redefine the organization's conceptual model without governed authorization.

No reasoning provider is RIP's authority, identity, or constitutional intelligence. A reasoning provider supplies bounded reasoning capability under RIP's governing artifacts.

## 10. Operations and Changes

Operations SHALL be derived from current organizational understanding, authority, dependencies, constraints, and evidence.

Changes SHALL be traceable from origin through proposal, authority, implementation, operational effect, and validation.

## 11. Evolution

Organizations SHALL preserve their history without becoming prisoners of past decisions.

Past decisions are evidence and history, not immutable law. Authority may evolve through the governed proposal process.

Governance precedes evolution. Learning, technical capability, or implementation convenience SHALL NOT bypass governed change.

## 12. Constitutional Purpose

Every directory, artifact, protocol, field, interface, relationship, capability, and automated behavior in RIP SHALL have an identifiable constitutional purpose.

Artifacts and capabilities without a defensible purpose SHOULD be removed, deferred, rejected, or clearly marked as experimental.

Every capability SHALL justify its existence by identifying the organizational purpose it serves and the authority under which it operates.

## 13. Deliverability

RIP SHALL be built as a usable system rather than an aspirational description.

Foundational artifacts SHALL be:

- inspectable;
- traceable;
- understandable;
- versioned;
- implementable;
- transferable;
- internally consistent;
- capable of validation.

## 14. Self-Governance and Self-Understanding

RIP SHALL apply this Constitution to its own evolution before applying its governing expectations to another organization.

RIP SHALL continuously cultivate an accurate understanding of its own purpose, authority, structure, capabilities, limitations, evidence, uncertainty, and history.

RIP SHALL NOT claim to understand another organization more deeply than it understands itself, and SHALL NOT conceal the limits of either understanding.

Its repository SHOULD NOT intentionally lag behind approved organizational understanding.

When a coherent body of decisions has been governed, the repository SHOULD be brought into alignment in a complete consistency pass rather than fragmented into artificial documentation phases.

## 15. Constitutional Corpus and Registry

RIP-007 — Constitutional Document Registry is the authoritative catalog of governed artifacts, their identities, classifications, status, authority, version, ownership, and lineage.

Every governed artifact SHALL be registered exactly once and SHALL remain consistent with its Registry entry.

The Registry does not create authority merely by listing an artifact; it records authority established through governance.

## 16. Amendment

This Constitution may be amended only through an approved RIP proposal.

Every amendment SHALL retain its proposal history, decision rationale, implementation record, validation result, and prior constitutional text.

No implementation convenience, technological advancement, provider capability, or operational expediency SHALL supersede this Constitution.
