## Mission

> RIP exists to preserve organizational knowledge by reducing or eliminating the loss of information, data, decisions, rationale, context, lessons, and institutional memory so that organizations do not have to rediscover what they once knew or repeatedly pay for the same lesson.

## Purpose

The Mission defines the enduring purpose of the Repository Intelligence Platform (RIP).

It is the canonical statement of why RIP exists and serves as the constitutional foundation for all governance, architecture, engineering, implementation, and future capabilities.

The Mission is intentionally stable, technology-independent, and organizationally focused.

## Scope

All governed artifacts, engineering decisions, implementations, and operational activities SHALL support and remain consistent with this Mission.

No implementation may supersede the Mission.

## Authority

This document derives its authority from the RIP Constitution and serves as the authoritative source for the platform's mission.

Any amendment to this Mission SHALL occur through constitutional governance.
