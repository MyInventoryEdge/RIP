## Register Rules

1. Entries are never deleted to conceal abandoned or unsuccessful work.
2. Corrections preserve the prior record.
3. Reopened proposals retain their original identity and history.
4. Superseding proposals identify what they replace.
5. Validation outcomes are recorded even when unfavorable.
6. Approval, implementation, and validation remain separate states.
7. Knowledge originating in conversation is preserved through governed artifacts when it has lasting organizational relevance.
