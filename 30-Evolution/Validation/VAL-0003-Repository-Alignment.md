# VAL-0003 — Repository Alignment

**Validation ID:** VAL-0003  
**Decision:** DEC-0003  
**Proposal:** RIP-PROP-0003  
**Status:** Initial Repository Validation Complete; Independent Assessment Pending  
**Validation Date:** 2026-07-28  
**Validator:** RIP Gatekeeper and authoring process  

## Validation Objective

Determine whether RIP's repository now reflects the approved product vision and platform direction.

## Criteria

The repository must communicate that:

- RIP is a governed organizational knowledge platform;
- the Organization is the primary object;
- Knowledge, Governance, and Execution are foundational pillars;
- conversations are not institutional memory;
- durable understanding moves through a governed lifecycle;
- knowledge retains provenance;
- supplied and inferred knowledge remain distinguishable;
- concepts are independent from providers;
- Operations derives from organizational understanding;
- Changes are traceable organizational events;
- AI and Hosts do not silently create authority;
- RIP governs its own evolution.

- RIP Vision

- Lexicon

- Governance
- Organizational Learning
- Proposal Template
- Proposal Lifecycle
- Decision Record
- Evolution Register

- Start Here onboarding guide

## Result

The foundational repository artifacts have been aligned with DEC-0003.

## Limitations

This validation confirms documentation and governance alignment. It does not claim that the executable platform, generated Operations, provider abstractions, organizational ingestion, or automated validation systems are implemented.

## Independent Validation Required

A new engineer or Cody should review the repository without receiving the originating conversation and explain:

1. what RIP is;
2. what its primary object is;
3. how knowledge becomes authority;
4. what AI and Hosts may and may not do;
5. how concepts and providers differ;
6. what remains to be implemented.

## Final State

**Validated with limitations pending independent assessment.**
