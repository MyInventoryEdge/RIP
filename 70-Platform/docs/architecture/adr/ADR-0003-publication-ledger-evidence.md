# ADR-0003 — Publication Ledger is immutable evidence, not commitment
**Status:** Accepted

**Question:** What does ledger existence mean?
**Context:** Interrupted records may remain persisted.
**Alternatives Considered:** Ledger equals commitment; ledger as evidence.
**Evidence:** JA-2B/JA-2C constitutional revisions.
**Decision:** The append-only ledger is immutable publication evidence only.
**Constitutional Justification:** Head History alone commits.
**Consequences:** Orphans may be dispositioned without history rewrite.
**Authorities Affected:** Journal Authority.
**Related Constitutional Evidence:** CE-JA-003, JA-2C.
