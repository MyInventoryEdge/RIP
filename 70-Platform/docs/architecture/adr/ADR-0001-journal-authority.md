# ADR-0001 — Journal Authority as a separate constitutional authority
**Status:** Accepted

**Question:** Who authenticates global publication history?
**Context:** Producer-domain logic could not honestly own shared persistence integrity.
**Alternatives Considered:** Producer-owned journals; one shared Journal Authority.
**Evidence:** ARB2-C-001; CE-JA-001 through CE-JA-003.
**Decision:** Journal Authority separately owns authenticated publication, head history, serialization, and validation.
**Constitutional Justification:** Shared evidence integrity must have one owner.
**Consequences:** Producers submit opaque typed evidence; they retain domain semantics.
**Authorities Affected:** Journal, Transaction, Trust.
**Related Constitutional Evidence:** CE-JA-001–003.
