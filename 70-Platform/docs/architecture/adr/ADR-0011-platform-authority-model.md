# ADR-0011 — Platform Authority Model
**Status:** Accepted

**Question:** What must exist before a component may be recognized as a Platform Authority?
**Context:** Platform capabilities require durable, historically meaningful, independently verifiable truth.
**Alternatives Considered:** Treat services, caches, or projections as authorities; require a constitutional authority model.
**Evidence:** CE-PLATFORM-001 and CE-PLATFORM-002.
**Decision:** Adopt [Platform Authority Model](../Platform-Authority-Model.md) as authoritative architecture.
**Constitutional Justification:** Truth ownership must be singular, durable, historically meaningful, independently verifiable, and fail closed.
**Consequences:** Future authorities require admission and constitutional evidence before implementation.
**Authorities Affected:** All current and future platform authorities.
**Related Constitutional Evidence:** CE-PLATFORM-001–002.
