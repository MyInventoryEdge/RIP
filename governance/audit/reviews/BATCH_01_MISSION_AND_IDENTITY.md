## Consolidation decision

### HDR-0001 and HDR-0002 — Consolidated under one canonical mission decision

HDR-0001 and HDR-0002 are closely related and shall not become competing or repetitive Decision Records.

The canonical decision is:

> **RIP exists to preserve organizational knowledge by reducing or eliminating the loss of information, data, decisions, rationale, context, lessons, and institutional memory so that organizations do not have to rediscover what they once knew or repeatedly pay for the same lesson.**

Governance, reasoning, AI, repository intelligence, automation, knowledge graphs, engineering workflows, and Platform Wisdom are mechanisms serving this mission. They are not substitutes for it.

**Traceability treatment:**

- `HDR-0001` remains the primary recovered-decision identifier.
- `HDR-0002` remains preserved as a supporting principle and alias in the audit trail.
- One canonical Mission Decision Record will represent both recovered items.
- Neither identifier is deleted, because consolidation must not erase historical traceability.

## Separately retained decisions

### HDR-0003 — The organization is RIP's primary object

**Status:** Ratified

Repositories, products, services, people, infrastructure, operations, providers, decisions, and history are modeled as parts of or relationships within the organization. A repository shall not silently replace the organization as RIP's platform boundary.

This remains separate because it establishes the platform's conceptual object model rather than merely restating the mission.

### HDR-0004 — RIP is an organizational knowledge-preservation, continuity, and governance platform

**Status:** Ratified with strengthened wording

> **RIP is a governed organizational knowledge-preservation, continuity, and governance platform. Repository intelligence is its first major specialization, not its permanent product boundary.**

This remains separate because it defines platform identity and product scope.
