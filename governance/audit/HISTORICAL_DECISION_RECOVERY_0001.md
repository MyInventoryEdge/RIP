## Purpose

Recover approved and materially accepted RIP decisions from prior project artifacts and the current governance discussion, classify each decision, and assign it to a permanent governed repository location.

This is a preservation audit, not a summary. A recovered decision is not complete until it is represented in the repository, reviewed, approved, and promoted through the governed change process.

## Audit rule

> No approved decision is left only in conversation.

Every recovered item must be:

1. institutionalized in a governed artifact;
2. explicitly superseded; or
3. intentionally rejected with preserved rationale.

## Recovered mission and identity decisions

### HDR-0001 — Preservation is the core mission

**State:** Approved  
**Decision:** RIP exists first to reduce or eliminate the loss of organizational information, decisions, rationale, context, lessons, and institutional memory. Governance, reasoning, AI, repository intelligence, and platform wisdom are mechanisms serving that mission, not substitutes for it.  
**Permanent destinations:** `MISSION.md`, Constitution, Decision Record.

### HDR-0002 — Organizations should not rediscover what they once knew

**State:** Approved  
**Decision:** RIP shall preserve enough evidence, context, rationale, authority, implementation history, and validation to prevent avoidable rediscovery and repeated payment for the same lesson.  
**Permanent destinations:** `MISSION.md`, Constitution, Organizational Learning.

### HDR-0003 — The organization is RIP's primary object

**State:** Previously approved and retained  
**Decision:** Repositories, products, services, people, infrastructure, operations, providers, decisions, and history are modeled as parts of or relationships within the organization. A repository shall not silently replace the organization as the platform boundary.  
**Permanent destinations:** Constitution, Architecture, Lexicon.

### HDR-0004 — RIP is an organizational continuity and governance platform

**State:** Approved direction  
**Decision:** Repository intelligence is an initial specialization. RIP's broader identity is a governed organizational knowledge and continuity platform.  
**Permanent destinations:** `MISSION.md`, root `README.md`, Architecture.

## Recovered constitutional decisions

### HDR-0005 — Reality precedes implementation

RIP models organizational reality and shall not redefine it for implementation convenience.

### HDR-0006 — Evidence precedes conclusion

Trusted knowledge and significant conclusions require traceable evidence, assumptions, reasoning, confidence, and uncertainty.

### HDR-0007 — Authority shall never be inferred

Usefulness, confidence, technical control, repetition, age, implementation status, or model output do not create authority.

### HDR-0008 — Understanding precedes automation

RIP shall understand purpose, structure, authority, evidence, constraints, and uncertainty before consequential recommendation or action.

### HDR-0009 — Governance precedes evolution

Learning, reasoning, automation, implementation convenience, or provider capability may not bypass governed change.

### HDR-0010 — Uncertainty shall be explicit

RIP and its Hosts shall expose missing evidence, limitations, conflicts, and uncertainty rather than conceal them.

### HDR-0011 — Institutional memory is an organizational asset

Ideas, proposals, evidence, assumptions, decisions, rationale, rejected and deferred alternatives, implementation outcomes, validation, reconsideration criteria, provenance, and relevant history shall be preserved.

### HDR-0012 — Every significant decision shall be explainable

A decision must remain traceable to evidence, assumptions, alternatives, rationale, authority, implementation, and validation.

### HDR-0013 — Every capability must justify its constitutional purpose

Directories, artifacts, protocols, fields, interfaces, relationships, capabilities, and automated behavior must identify the organizational purpose and authority they serve.

### HDR-0014 — RIP governs itself first

RIP shall apply its own constitutional and governance expectations to its development before applying them to another organization.

### HDR-0015 — Human and organizational authority remain final

RIP may observe, infer, reason, recommend, draft, simulate, and validate. It shall not silently govern, approve its own proposals, amend constitutional authority, or convert recommendations into policy.

### HDR-0016 — Conversation is not institutional memory

Important understanding must be promoted from transient conversation into durable governed artifacts.

### HDR-0017 — Canonical knowledge

Every governing fact has one authoritative home. Other representations are derived, indexed, generated, or historical.

### HDR-0018 — Platform independence and clean institutional memory

Customer instances shall not inherit RIP engineering history or another organization's knowledge. Each organization begins with its own governed institutional memory.

## Recovered knowledge-domain decisions

### HDR-0019 — Three knowledge domains

RIP distinguishes:

1. **Platform knowledge** — reusable capabilities, generalized methods, templates, workflows, validation methods, and governed platform wisdom.
2. **Engineering knowledge** — RIP's internal development history, architecture evolution, decisions, audits, and implementation records.
3. **Organizational knowledge** — a customer's facts, terminology, governance, systems, people, repositories, decisions, and institutional memory.

These domains must remain architecturally and operationally separated.

### HDR-0020 — Platform learns patterns, not customer facts

The platform may retain generalized methods, discovery patterns, heuristics, workflows, and validated reusable wisdom. It shall not retain or redistribute organization-specific facts as platform knowledge.

### HDR-0021 — Memory belongs to the organization; wisdom belongs to the platform

Organizational memory remains owned by its organization. Generalized, de-identified, reviewed, and approved reusable methods may enter Platform Wisdom.

### HDR-0022 — Platform evolution is governed

Candidate wisdom requires evidence, abstraction, privacy and boundary review, validation, human approval, versioning, and release governance before entering the platform.

## Recovered reasoning and Host decisions

### HDR-0023 — Reasoning is provider-independent

No reasoning provider is RIP's authority, identity, or constitutional intelligence. Providers supply bounded reasoning capability under RIP governance.

### HDR-0024 — Required reasoning flow

Reasoning proceeds through distinguishable stages:

```text
Observation -> Interpretation -> Recommendation -> Governance -> Decision -> Implementation
```

Reasoning may not silently collapse these stages.

### HDR-0025 — Host is a constitutional executor

A Host interprets and enacts approved authority, identifies missing context, preserves traceability, exposes uncertainty, and operates within explicit capabilities. A Host does not create authority.

### HDR-0026 — Continuity before creativity

Before proposing new foundational analysis or recommendations, RIP should recover relevant prior decisions, identify current authority, detect supersession or conflict, and begin from established institutional knowledge.

## Recovered engineering and lifecycle decisions

### HDR-0027 — Repository is the institution

The repository is the canonical durable source for RIP governance, architecture, engineering decisions, validation, and history. Conversation is a discovery workspace, not the final authority.

### HDR-0028 — Capture before continue

When discussion produces a coherent body of approved decisions, discovery pauses until those decisions are captured, reviewed, approved, and preserved.

### HDR-0029 — A decision is incomplete until institutionalized

Agreement in conversation is not completion. Completion requires a governed record, repository location, review, approval, and traceable implementation/validation status.

### HDR-0030 — Governance Sprint workflow

Governance work follows:

```text
Produce -> Review -> Approve -> Commit
```

The sprint is not complete merely because documents exist; recovered decisions must be traceable and gaps explicitly recorded.

### HDR-0031 — Governed engineering lifecycle

The accepted lifecycle is:

```text
Vision -> Proposal -> Architecture -> Governance Review -> Implementation -> Testing -> Validation -> Approval -> Release -> Chronicle
```

The exact state model requires standards work because decision state, implementation status, and validation status must remain distinct.

### HDR-0032 — Governance gates cannot be silently bypassed

Proposal, architecture, implementation, validation, and release each require explicit evidence and approval appropriate to their authority.

### HDR-0033 — Nothing important lives only in conversation

Significant architectural, governance, product, and engineering conclusions must be promoted into governed artifacts.

### HDR-0034 — Every major directory is self-describing

Each major directory shall contain a `README.md` stating Purpose, Authority, Contents, Relationships, and explicit Rules or exclusions.

## Recovered product and workflow decisions from the initial handoff

### HDR-0036 — Inventory Edge is the reference implementation, not the product boundary

Inventory Edge organization-specific knowledge belongs in an Organization Profile and shall not be embedded in the universal platform.

### HDR-0037 — Developer Studio is a Repository Session Host

Developer Studio is not the platform itself. It is the first Host implementing RIP's session and governance contracts.

### HDR-0038 — Engineering sessions begin consistently

Every governed engineering session begins through a common Repository Session bootstrap rather than ad hoc prompting.

### HDR-0039 — Engineering intent is determined before implementation

A session must determine intent, applicable authority, owning project, required context, validation, documentation impact, and mandatory stop points before implementation begins.

### HDR-0040 — Repository Session is first; dashboard is secondary

The minimum useful Host begins with the Repository Session experience. Dashboards are later operational views, not the primary workflow.

### HDR-0041 — Knowledge Reflection occurs after validation

After implementation and validation, RIP evaluates whether the work revealed durable organizational knowledge, repository friction, missing documentation, reusable patterns, or invalidated authority.

### HDR-0042 — Knowledge Improvement Proposals require evidence

KIPs may be drafted from observed engineering evidence. They shall not automatically modify authority or documentation, and speculative or stylistic opinions alone are insufficient.

### HDR-0043 — Every governed engineering session produces a Session Record

Session Records preserve task, intent, authorities consulted, context, validation, documentation changes, proposals generated, and completion summary.

## Current audit findings

### AF-0001 — Conversion failure

The project generated substantial valuable discussion but institutionalized too little of it. The failure is not idea quality; it is incomplete conversion from discussion to governed repository artifacts.

### AF-0002 — Mission wording lag

The existing repository strongly emphasizes preservation and organizational knowledge, but the newly reaffirmed driving mission—reducing or eliminating loss of data, decisions, rationale, context, and institutional memory—is not yet stated at the forefront in a canonical `MISSION.md`.

### AF-0003 — Structural conflict

The newly approved target repository structure differs from the current numeric repository map. A migration decision and preservation plan are required; silently replacing the existing structure would violate RIP's own continuity principles.

### AF-0004 — Decision traceability remains incomplete

Prior audits identified approved items without complete proposal/decision records and unclear authority status. Governance Sprint 0001 must backfill canonical Decision Records rather than treating an index as the decision itself.

### AF-0005 — Gatekeeper authority requires explicit designation

The Gatekeeper role is defined, but the repository still requires an inspectable authorization and delegation model identifying who or what body holds which scope of authority.

### AF-0006 — Lifecycle dimensions require separation

Decision state, implementation status, and validation status must be modeled independently. Approval does not imply implementation; implementation does not imply validation.

### AF-0007 — Standards remain incomplete

Governance Sprint 0001 must establish standards for identifiers, statuses, authority scope, artifact schemas, traceability, correction/supersession, testing, security, storage, release, and Host conformance.

### AF-0008 — Historical coverage is not yet complete

This pass recovered the current conversation and the available founding/handoff artifacts. It does not claim every historical chat has been reviewed. Further supplied conversations or handoffs must be audited against this register before Sprint 0001 receives final validation.
